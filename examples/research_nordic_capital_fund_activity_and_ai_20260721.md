# Research Report: Nordic Capital — Recent Fund Activity & AI/Data-Team Build-Out

## Executive Summary

Nordic Capital is in an unusually active phase. It is raising its **twelfth flagship fund (Fund XII, €10bn target)** alongside a new **€500m Article 9 impact fund ("Elevate")**, having reached a combined **€6.2bn first close in November 2025**, with final close targeted for 2026. In 2025 it deployed **~€3.4bn across eight platform investments** and generated **~€3.1bn from two landmark exits** (the NOBA Bank Group IPO and the ~$9.4bn Clario sale to Thermo Fisher). Stated AUM sits around **€39bn** (per its own site; other sources cite €31–34bn — see discrepancy flag). On AI, the evidence is **materially stronger than expected**: Nordic Capital has a named **Head of Data Platform (Simon Rosemarin, joined Apr 2024)**, an **Operations Data Director (Gustav Sandborgh)** self-tagged "Data & AI", a **CTO (Mark Peacock)**, a proprietary portfolio-monitoring platform called **"Cockpit"**, an internal **data lake**, a firm-wide **"AI task force"**, and an annual internal **"Tech & Data Summit"**. It is clearly building an internal data/AI capability right now — though it remains **behind EQT's decade-old Motherbrain** in maturity.

**Signal Strength verdict: 4 / 5** (strong public evidence of an active build-out; not a 5 only because there is no single "we're hiring a data-science team" announcement, no disclosed headcount, and the effort is still mid-journey). Detail at the end.

## OBJECTIVE 1 — Recent Fund Activity

### 1.1 Latest fund cycle — Fund XII + Elevate

- **Fund XII (flagship buyout).** Target **€10bn**; fundraising launched ~April–June 2025. By **17 Sept 2025** it had raised **€5bn (~50% of target)**, nearing a first close [Source: bloomberg.com; pe-insights.com]. Deal size range is **€250m–€1bn equity checks across four sectors** [Source: privateequityinternational.com]. If successful it "would rank among the largest European buyout funds currently in the market" [Source: pe-insights.com]. **Final close targeted 2026.**
  - *Note on target size:* Bloomberg/PE-Insights cite **€10bn**; Swedish daily Di (via AMWatch) frames it as **>SEK 100bn ≈ €9.1bn** [Source: amwatch.com]. Fund XI closed at €9bn in 2022, so Fund XII is a step-up in that range.
- **Combined first close.** On **20 Nov 2025**, a letter to investors (seen by Bloomberg) reported **€6.2bn raised at first close for Fund XII *and* the Elevate companion vehicle combined** [Source: placera.se]. An exact split between the two was not disclosed.
- **"Elevate" (new impact fund).** A new **Article 9 climate/impact fund**, target **~€500m** (Di cites SEK 6.5bn ≈ €592m), designed to **sit alongside Fund XII** [Source: impactloop.com; amwatch.com]. It focuses on **decarbonisation, fossil-free solutions and circularity** and had secured **~€250m at first close** [Source: impactloop.com]. Elin Ljung (Head of Sustainability & Communications) is a named spokesperson.
- **Evolution II (mid-market).** Nordic Capital's second mid-market fund closed at a **€2bn hard cap** after a "rapid fundraise," announced ~**Dec 2024 / early 2025** [Source: prnewswire.com headline; nordiccapital.com/about-us; news.cision.com]. Described as ~65% larger than Evolution I (€1.2bn) [Source: grokipedia.com — unverified].

**Current fund family (from Nordic Capital's own About Us page):** Fund XI (€9.0bn, 2022) and Evolution I & II (€1.2bn + €2.0bn) are the active investing vehicles; 13 funds raised since 1990 [Source: nordiccapital.com/about-us].

### 1.3 Recent exits / IPOs (2025–2026)

- **NOBA Bank Group — IPO on Nasdaq Stockholm (Sept 2025).** One of the two "landmark realisations" of 2025; a multi-year transformation from monoline lender [Source: nordiccapital.com Annual Review 2025]. *(An AK&M article reports an earlier IPO postponement amid market volatility, valuing NOBA at ~€4bn; this appears to predate the eventual successful 2025 listing — flagged as sequencing ambiguity)* [Source: akm.ru].
- **Clario — sale to Thermo Fisher for up to $9.4bn** ($8.9bn cash + up to $525m earn-out), from Nordic Capital and Astorg. Called "the largest full healthcare private-equity exit announced globally in 2025"; **expected to close mid-2026** [Source: pe-insights.com; nordiccapital.com Annual Review 2025].
- **Cint Group — full exit (sold remaining shares) ~Feb 2026** [Source: scout candidate discoperi.com — not independently fetched; treat as lower confidence].
- **Cary Group — exit process underway (€3bn+).** Co-owned with CVC (taken private 2022); exploring sale or IPO, described as "early stage" with "no certainty" [Source: pe-insights.com].
- **Max Matthiessen — partial sale (Nov 2024), NOT a full exit.** Sold a stake to a **Canadian pension fund**; Nordic Capital **remains a co-owner** (majority owner since 2020). Financial Services advisory [Source: borsen.dk]. *(Predates the 2025–26 window and is a recap, not an exit — flagged.)*
- Combined, the two 2025 landmark realisations (NOBA IPO + Clario sale) generated **~€3.1bn in exit value** [Source: nordiccapital.com Annual Review 2025].

### 1.4 AUM, dry powder, trajectory

- **AUM: €39bn** per Nordic Capital's own About Us page (current) — with **€29.5bn invested in 155+ companies since inception**, **57 current portfolio companies**, **100 exits**, **€18bn portfolio revenues**, **81,000 people employed** across the portfolio [Source: nordiccapital.com/about-us].
- **⚠️ AUM discrepancy:** Third-party sources cite **€34bn** (pe-insights.com, pe-insights Clario piece), **€31bn / ~$40bn** (akm.ru, internally inconsistent), and **~€33–34bn** (grokipedia.com, internally inconsistent). The **€39bn** figure is the most authoritative (primary source) and likely the most recent. Treat sub-€35bn figures as older snapshots.
- **Dry powder:** Not explicitly disclosed by any source. With a €6.2bn+ first close on Fund XII/Elevate plus Evolution II's €2bn, available capital is substantial but no clean number was published — **could not verify a specific dry-powder figure.**
- **Trajectory:** LTM EBITDA growth ~20% across the portfolio in 2025; ~80% of value creation driven by earnings growth historically [Source: nordiccapital.com]. The firm is scaling both flagship and adjacent vehicles (mid-market Evolution, impact Elevate) — a **product-diversification trajectory**.

### 2.2 Stated AI strategy & internal tooling (official "Voices of Nordic Capital" series, 2026)

- **Proprietary platform "Cockpit"** — internal **portfolio-monitoring platform** built on structured ERP data from portfolio companies; Nordic Capital is "introducing generative AI to handle unstructured data" next [Source: nordiccapital.com "AI to sharpen deal decisions," 3 Jun 2026, interview with CTO Mark Peacock].
- **Internal data lake + deal-memo archive** — "a substantial archive of deal memos, analyses and outcome data, most of it unstructured," which the firm plans to interrogate with AI and connect to structured data in "Nordic Capital's own **data lake**" to "surface patterns in how decisions were made, where value was created" [Source: nordiccapital.com, Peacock interview].
- **Deal sourcing / due diligence:** "Early-stage screening is where the impact is likely to be most significant... A **bid/no-bid decision that currently takes up to two weeks could... be brought down to a matter of days**" [Source: nordiccapital.com, Peacock interview]. Strategic framing: "AI is not just an efficiency lever, but part of how value is created and how investors differentiate."
- **Firm-wide "AI task force"** — confirmed by CFO Jesper Söderberg: "We have an **AI task force** that I am part of, which helps coordinate how the firm uses these tools," plus AI governance guidelines and "**25 must-dos on cybersecurity**" for portfolio companies [Source: nordiccapital.com "AI adoption in the CFO function," 26 May 2026].
- **Portfolio value creation:** "**More than 20 portfolio companies already have AI embedded directly into their products**"; Nordic Capital runs "**AI maturity assessments across the full portfolio**" and supports a "peer community of **more than 100 chief technology, product and information officers**." Named examples: Zafin, ArisGlobal, Siteimprove, Foxway, Boost.ai [Source: nordiccapital.com "AI and active ownership in practice," 30 Apr 2026].
- **Due-diligence lens:** In wealth-management deals, Nordic Capital assesses a target's "technology stack and digital maturity... how well it uses data... how prepared it is to integrate AI-driven tools" [Source: nordiccapital.com, Emil Anderson interview, 18 May 2026].
- **Internal events:** An annual **"Tech & Data Summit" (Zurich)** and an annual **"Digital Summit"** (Mikael Carlsson keynote on AI/digital value creation) [Source: LinkedIn posts — login-walled, so event *existence* is from the scout's snippets/titles, not fully verified body text].
- **Historical anchor (2020):** Nordic Capital partnered with **Capgemini** on a "Digitalization Toolbox" to drive AI/ML in portfolio companies — but that used **Capgemini's** data-science team, not an internal Nordic Capital one [Source: nordiccapital.com, 13 Jul 2020]. The shift to *internal* capability (Cockpit, data lake, Head of Data Platform) is the 2024–2026 development.

### 2.3 Competitive read vs. EQT (Motherbrain)

**Nordic Capital is on-par-and-rising among mainstream buyout GPs, but clearly behind EQT.** EQT's **Motherbrain launched in 2016** as a dedicated AI/data team spanning the full investment lifecycle (sourcing bolt-ons, VC deal flow, advisor matching), and now has a **"Motherbrain Labs"** consulting arm advising portfolio companies on AI [Source: ionanalytics.com; eqtgroup.com — official pages were cookie-walled but corroborated by ION Analytics]. That is a **~decade head-start**. Nordic Capital's comparable internal assets — the Cockpit platform, a data lake, a Head of Data Platform (hired Apr 2024) and an AI task force — are **~1–2 years old and still mid-build** (CTO Peacock explicitly frames it as "cycle two"). So: **EQT = pioneer/leader; Nordic Capital = fast-follower actively standing up the capability now, ahead of many peers who are still at the "off-the-shelf productivity tools" stage** that BCG flags as the Nordic-region norm [Source: bcg.com "Nordic AI Inflection Point," 10 Mar 2026]. Broader industry context: 83% of PE investors now want AI-readiness in targets, but only 40% of evaluated companies meet the bar [Source: ionanalytics.com, citing Bain].

## Signal Strength Verdict: **4 / 5**

**How strong is the evidence that Nordic Capital is genuinely standing up an internal AI/data-science team right now? → 4 out of 5 (strong).**

**Why a 4:**
- ✅ A named, dedicated **"Head of Data Platform" (Simon Rosemarin) hired April 2024** — a hire made specifically to build data infrastructure.
- ✅ A named **"Operations Data Director" (Gustav Sandborgh)** in the value-creation team, self-describing as "Data & AI".
- ✅ A **CTO (Mark Peacock)** publicly articulating a concrete, multi-phase internal AI roadmap.
- ✅ Proprietary internal assets named on the record: the **"Cockpit"** platform and a **data lake**.
- ✅ A firm-wide **"AI task force"** confirmed by the CFO.
- ✅ Multiple **official 2026 publications** ("Voices of Nordic Capital" series) detailing internal AI use for deal screening, portfolio monitoring, and knowledge management.
- ✅ Internal **"Tech & Data Summit"** signalling a standing function.

**Why not a 5:**
- ❌ **No single announcement** explicitly stating "we are building an in-house data-science team," and **no disclosed team headcount** — the function is inferred from individual bios + strategy articles, and appears **distributed** across two advisory teams rather than a consolidated unit.
- ❌ The effort is **explicitly mid-journey** (CTO frames it as "cycle two"), so this is a capability being *stood up*, not yet a mature platform like EQT's Motherbrain.

**Bottom line:** The evidence that Nordic Capital is actively building internal AI/data-science capability *right now* is strong and well-triangulated across official primary sources. What's missing is only the "org-announcement" layer (a headline hire-a-team press release and headcount), which keeps it just short of a 5.
